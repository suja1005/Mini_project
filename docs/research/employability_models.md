# Employability & Decision Models Research

## Research Foundations & Mathematical Formulations

### 1. Evidence-Based Skill Confidence
Self-reported skill does not equal verified employability. The system computes estimated skill confidence through multi-modal validation:
$$\text{Skill Confidence} = (\text{Self-Declaration} \times 0.10) + (\text{Assessment} \times 0.40) + (\text{Project Evidence} \times 0.30) + (\text{Practical Evidence} \times 0.20)$$

### 2. Multi-Career Recommendation Score
$$\text{Career Fit} = (S_{\text{tech}} \times 0.40) + (S_{\text{interest}} \times 0.20) + (S_{\text{aptitude}} \times 0.15) + (S_{\text{academic}} \times 0.10) + (S_{\text{preference}} \times 0.15)$$

### 3. Career Risk & Achievability Intelligence
Achievability is a model-based metric reflecting the feasibility of reaching target readiness given current skill distance, learning effort, and student aptitude:
$$\text{Achievability} = f(\text{Fit Score}, \text{Employability Gap}, \text{Learning Effort}, \text{Risk Index})$$

*Note: All scores are model-based algorithmic estimates and do not constitute employment guarantees.*
